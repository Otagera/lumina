# VC Assessment — Lumina

*Honest 10-minute demo take. What lands, what would give a VC pause.*

---

## What's genuinely strong

- **The core value prop is crisp**: scan QR → selfie → find yourself in every photo. That's a 10-second pitch any wedding photographer understands.
- **Multi-vertical from day one** (weddings, church, corporate, schools) is smart — not boxed into one niche while still showing clear use cases.
- **The theme editor** shows you've thought about the host experience, not just the guest experience. That's defensible.
- **Self-serve demo on the homepage** is the right call. VCs can evaluate without scheduling a call.

---

## What would give me pause

**Moat question**
Face search on event photos is the thesis, but AWS Rekognition and Google Photos already do this. The pitch needs to answer "why can't the photographer just use [X]?" more explicitly. The answer is probably "no-friction guest delivery" — but that's not front and center on the homepage.

**Pricing lacks context**
Tiers need a hook that anchors the value. "How many albums does a working wedding photographer shoot per year? 40? The Pro tier pays for itself on the first wedding." Without that framing, numbers feel arbitrary.

**Trust/privacy above the fold**
Face biometrics are a regulatory minefield (BIPA, GDPR). There's one disclaimer in the demo section ("selfie never stored"). A VC will immediately ask: "What's your data retention policy and what's your exposure if a photographer operates in Illinois?" A visible privacy stance is needed — not fine print.

**The hero doesn't show a real outcome**
It shows the product. The strongest hook for this category is a guest's reaction — the "oh my god, it found me in 47 photos" moment. That's what sells it to hosts. Right now the hero leads with features.

**B2B vs B2C tension**
Are you selling to photographers/event hosts (B2B) or to guests (B2C consumer)? The homepage mixes both frames. VCs want to know who writes the check and why.

---

## The one thing to fix before any investor conversation

Replace the hero headline with a guest outcome, then make the photographer/host the buyer in the subhead.

> **Current framing**: feature-led
>
> **Better framing**:
> - Headline: *"Your guests find themselves in every photo — automatically."*
> - Subhead: *"The AI photo platform event hosts use to delight every guest."*

That orders the story correctly: guest benefit → host motivation → your product.

---

## Summary

The bones are solid. The codebase is clean, the infra is production-ready, and the self-serve demo does real work. The gap is narrative architecture — the homepage is feature-led when it needs to be outcome-led. Fix the hero framing and add an explicit privacy stance before any investor conversation.
